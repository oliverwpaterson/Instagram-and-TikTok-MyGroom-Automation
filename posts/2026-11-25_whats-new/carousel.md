# What's new in MyGroom (iPhone 1.2 release carousel)

Made 24 Nov 2026, after the owner confirmed the iPhone update was approved on the App Store. Features from playbooks/mygroom-features.md. There are no app screenshots of the new features yet, so the slides use approved library photos and describe the features in text only.

## Slides
1. Cover: **What's new in MyGroom** / 6 new things, now on iPhone and web
2. **Knows if he runs hot** / Tell the Rug Advisor if your horse runs hot or cold, then see exactly why it picked that rug.
3. **Reminders for every date** / Farrier, jabs, worming, dentist, MOT, insurance. Choose how many days' notice you want.
4. **A log your sharer sees** / Set training goals and log each session, so your sharer knows what's been worked on.
5. **A list for the feed shop** / A shopping list, so nothing gets left behind at the feed store. You asked, we built it.
6. **Rabbits welcome too** / Add rabbits, guinea pigs and more, with care, meds, documents and food, next to your cat.
7. **Now in 10 languages** / English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch, Polish, Romanian, Swedish and Danish.
8. End: **Update your app, or try it free** / Your turn: Which one will you use first?
