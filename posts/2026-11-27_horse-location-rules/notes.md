## Facts (checked 27 Nov 2026)
- Defra, "Equine sector: preparing for changes" (published 20 Nov 2026) https://www.gov.uk/guidance/equine-sector-preparing-for-changes : under the UK-EU SPS Agreement, owners will record where each horse is normally kept on the Central Equine Database and "update this information when the horse's usual location changes for more than 30 days"; premises where equines are kept, "including private residences", must be registered or approved; rules apply "from 1 March 2028 subject to UK and EU procedures"; detailed guidance "by summer 2027"; "You are not expected to make changes now." Also new microchip numbering and ID document formats ("continue using existing identification documents unless they are told..."), not used in the reel.
- Horse & Hound, 23 Nov 2026 https://www.horseandhound.co.uk/industry-news/uk-eu-agreement-all-horses-locations-britain : owners "do not need to do anything yet".
- Your Horse https://www.yourhorse.co.uk/news/new-rules-will-see-change-across-the-equine-sector-from-single-horse-owners-to-large-scale-operations/ : World Horse Welfare says whether field-to-field moves are covered "is not yet clear". (Its "next year" wording conflicts with Defra's 2028 date; we followed Defra.)
- Applies to Great Britain; Northern Ireland separate.
