# What works on each platform (MyGroom, from our own Metricool data)

Built 25 Nov 2026 from the last 30 days of posts (26 Oct – 25 Nov 2026) at the owner's request, so each platform gets content customised to what works there. Small accounts and small samples: treat these as working rules, and update this file when the weekly analysis shows something different. Numbers are Metricool views per post (Instagram reels: IGRE23; Instagram feed: IGPO28; TikTok: TKPO07).

## Instagram (@mygroomltd)
| Format | Posts | Median views | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reels, first-person / "me" / "when you're…" text on screen | 16 | 138 | Average skip rate 42%. Best: "my fridge calendar has officially retired" 1,534 views, skip 25.7%; "the yard has no signal…" 579, skip 30.5% |
| Reels, numbered tips / advice with voiceover | 6 | 109 | Average skip rate 66% (wet dog 78%, cats and cars 84%). People swipe away in the first seconds |
| Reel, AgentOpus story | 1 | 232 | Skip 50.8%; the only reel with a comment, a save and a share |
| Feed posts (carousels and single images) | 15 | 31 | Reach is tiny compared with reels, but memes get the comments (GTA meme: 4 comments on 39 views) |

Rules for Instagram:
1. **Reels carry the reach.** Every Instagram reel is first-person and relatable: a "me / my / when you're…" line readable on frame one, the app shown as part of the owner's own routine. No numbered tip lists in Instagram reels.
2. **Feed posts are for followers, not reach.** Keep posting carousels and memes to the feed (they build the profile and earn saves and comments), but write the caption for people who already follow: save prompt plus comment question.
3. **Best times:** 10:00 is Instagram's strongest hour every weekday, 18:00 second (Metricool best-time scores for this account).
4. Caption: hook line first, 5 hashtags (Instagram's cap), first comment repeats the question.

## TikTok (@mygroomltd)
| Format | Posts | Median views | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Photo carousels / photo memes | 16 | 377 | Best: horse cost carousel 1,193; "the rug decision starts before you open the stable door" 1,094; winter yard check 556; Outlook meme 471 with 10 shares |
| Videos, tips / seasonal advice | 6 | 477 | Late Oct–early Nov: mud fever 782, frost 760, dark dog walks 695 (later ones ~250) |
| Videos, first-person text lines | 14 | 252 | Most stall around 220–300 views. Exceptions: "my fridge calendar has officially retired" 1,375, the AgentOpus story 608 |
| One-off app demo ("Bo's whole care routine at a glance") | 1 | 6,979 | Real app screens of one horse's routine; hasn't been repeated yet |

Rules for TikTok:
1. **Photo carousels are the most reliable format.** Numbers, money, checklists and "lists of things owners know" do best (costs, rug planning, brands). Aim for two photo carousels a week on TikTok.
2. **Tip and advice content works on TikTok**, unlike Instagram, so numbered tips and seasonal advice go to TikTok (as a photo carousel or a TikTok-only video).
3. **Memes as photo posts** (JPEG) for shares.
4. **Test an app-routine video again**: real screenshots of one animal's routine in sequence, like the "Bo" post.
5. Photo-post title 90 characters max; put the longer copy and 3–5 TikTok hashtags (#horsetok #equestrian plus topic tags) in the description. Best times: 10:00 and 18:00.
