## New in November 2026
- **10 languages:** English, Danish, Dutch, French, German, Italian, Polish, Romanian, Spanish and Swedish. Pick yours in Profile, or set your country and the app suggests it.
- **Personal Rug Advisor:** tell it whether your horse runs hot or cold, and whether they're in an enclosed or open-fronted stable, and it adjusts every recommendation. Each recommendation shows a "feels like" breakdown of why. Gentler advice when clip, wind and rain combine (under-rugging is preferred to over-rugging).
- **Your own rug rules:** set your own temperature-to-rug table and the Rug Advisor follows it.
- **"What's on now":** record which rug your horse is wearing, so anyone helping out (Shared Access) can check at a glance.
- **Rug advice anywhere:** set the location by town or village in any country, not just a UK postcode. Only the general area is stored, never the exact address.
- **Ready at the yard:** rug forecasts refresh for all your horses when you open the app, so the advice is there even with no signal.
- **Reminders for everything with a due date (push notifications):** farrier, vaccinations, worming, dentist, physio, grooming, medication courses, MOT, vehicle service, insurance renewals, foaling and calendar events. Switch each one on next to the record and choose how many days' notice you want. (Say "as many days' notice as you like"; don't invent a specific timing like "an hour before".)
- **Training goals and session log:** set goals, log each session, and your sharer (Shared Access) sees what's been worked on.
- **Shopping list:** a simple household list, from Home and Profile, so nothing gets left behind at the feed store. It came from a real user's feedback: "I always walk into the store knowing what I need and somehow I ALWAYS leave something behind." (Fine to quote anonymously; never name or invent the person.)
- **Other animals:** rabbits, guinea pigs and more, with care, medications, documents and food tracking.
- **Quick search:** tap the search icon to jump to any animal or page.
- **Feeding:** "scoop" as a unit, plus over 100 more feeds and supplements in the catalogue.
- **Hay calculator:** always leaves hay for the hours your horse is off grass (it no longer shows 0 kg when grazing covers the calories). Before quoting any forage-gap rule (e.g. "no more than 4–6 hours without forage"), check it against current BHS guidance in that run.
- **US English:** "blanket" instead of "rug" for US users.
- Reliability: push notifications now arrive reliably on iPhone; fixes for a black screen, saves after backgrounding, password reset links and long rides/walks resuming. (Fine for a "we listened" post, not a headline.)
