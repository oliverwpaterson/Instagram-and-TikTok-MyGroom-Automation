# 2026-W47 carousel: 6 things horse owners forget (until it's too late)

Topic and why: the theme's week-1 carousel. Save-worthy "what to check" list that complements the reels (reels show one problem each; the carousel covers six). TikTok photo posts were our best TikTok format last week, so this goes to TikTok photo mode as well.

Slides:
1. Cover: "6 things horse owners forget" / "(until it's too late). How many have caught you out?"
2. The flu jab due date: Pony Club and competitions check the passport. Out of date can mean no entry.
3. The next farrier visit: Six weeks slips to nine without anyone noticing. Book the next one before they leave.
4. Worming or worm count: Many vets now suggest a worm count first. Note when the last one was done.
5. The dentist check: Most horses need their teeth checked at least once a year. Ask your vet or EDT.
6. The horsebox MOT: Easy to forget until the week of the show. Check the date before you plan any trips.
7. The last scoop: Sunday night, last scoop of the supplement, shop shut. Keep an eye on what's left.
8. End: "Save this for your next yard visit" / "Which one always catches you out?"
