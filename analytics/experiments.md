# Weekly experiments

Started 25 Nov 2026 at the owner's request: "we need to sample and try some new things also each week".
Each Sunday run picks the next idea from the backlog (or a better one from that week's research), runs it as ONE clearly labelled experiment, and the following Sunday judges it against the baseline in `playbooks/platform-insights.md`. Change one thing at a time so the result means something.

How to judge (next Sunday's run):
- Compare with the median for the same network and format in platform-insights.md (Instagram reels ~138 views, skip ~42%; Instagram feed ~31; TikTok photos ~377; TikTok first-person videos ~252), plus comments, shares, saves and sign-ups that week.
- **Win** = clearly beats the median (roughly 1.5x views, or clearly more comments/saves/shares). Two wins in a row → move it into platform-insights.md as a rule.
- **Lose** = clearly below median. Note why, and don't repeat for at least 4 weeks.
- **Unclear** = re-run once with a different topic.

## Backlog (ideas to try, roughly in order)
1. **Instagram Trial Reel with a new hook style** (on top of the two standing weekly trial reels below): e.g. a 7–10 s loop or a third-person "she…" line as a Trial Reel. Metricool supports `TRIAL_REEL`.
2. **Aesthetic "she…" photo carousel (TikTok)**: 6–8 striking photos, one third-person line each ("she books the farrier before he's left the yard"), soft MyGroom mention at the end, #pinterestaesthetic. Inspired by @mkequest.
3. **App routine video**: one animal's routine through real screenshots, like "Bo's whole care routine at a glance" (6,979 TikTok views, never repeated).
4. **7–10 second looping reel**: one relatable line over a single still, music only, no voiceover.
5. **Same idea, two formats**: one topic as a TikTok carousel and as a reel, same day, to see which format wins for that topic.
6. **Different posting time**: move one reel to 10:00 (Instagram's top hour) instead of 18:00.
7. **Series post ("Part 1")**: a two-part story or list where part 2 comes 2–3 days later, to see if people come back.
8. **Comment-reply video**: answer a real comment or question-box answer from the previous week (only with a real comment; never invent one).
9. **Dog- or cat-only week**: shift the topic mix to test whether pet owners outside horses respond better.
10. **Seasonal or trend tie-in within 24 hours** of a UK news or calendar moment (like the Outlook outage meme: 10 shares).

## Instagram Trial Reels (standing, from 25 Nov 2026)
The owner asked on 25 Nov 2026 to use Trial Reels. Every week: two extra Instagram Trial Reels (shown to non-followers first). Trial 1 = one of that week's reels re-rendered with a different hook (hook test). Trial 2 = a finished reel that never went out on Instagram, or a strong past reel that's still seasonal (reach test; not the same reel twice within 8 weeks). Judge each against the Instagram reel median (~138 views, skip ~42%) and, for hook tests, against the main reel it was paired with. A hook that clearly wins (about 1.5x views or 10+ points lower skip) is used for the next week's main reels.
